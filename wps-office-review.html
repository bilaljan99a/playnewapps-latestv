2. What is WPS Office?

WPS Office (originally known as Writer, Presentation, and Spreadsheets) is an all-in-one office productivity software developed by Kingsoft Office. Founded in 1988, Kingsoft released the first version of WPS to provide a lightweight, high-speed document processing engine. Over thirty-eight years of continuous engineering refinement have transformed WPS into a global productivity ecosystem.

Unlike traditional office applications that force users to juggle multiple disjointed windows for documents, spreadsheets, and slides, WPS Office introduced the industry-pioneering Tabbed Document Interface (TDI). In WPS Office, every open document — whether a 50-page Word document, a multi-sheet Excel workbook, a pitch deck, or a PDF contract — opens as a clean tab within a single master application window, dramatically improving multitasking efficiency.

WPS Writer is the flagship word processing engine of the suite. Engineered as a direct alternative to Microsoft Word, WPS Writer supports native file reading and writing for .docx, .doc, .txt, .rtf, .dotx, and .html formats without distorting margins or page layouts.

Writer includes sophisticated editorial capabilities required by professional authors and students alike:

  • Advanced Typography & Formatting Controls: Precise line spacing, kerning, multi-column layouts, custom headers, footers, and table of contents generation.
  • Track Changes & Revision Collaboration: Reviewer markup with inline comment threads, strike-through revisions, and document compare modes identical to Microsoft Word.
  • Integrated Reference & Citation Tools: Endnote, footnote, bibliographical citation manager, and mail merge integration for bulk correspondence.
  • AI Writing Polisher: Real-time grammar checking, stylistic polishing, tone adjustment, and vocabulary enhancement (reducing reliance on external tools like Grammarly).

9. WPS PDF Suite & OCR Engine

One of WPS Office's strongest competitive advantages over Microsoft Office is its standalone, full-featured PDF Suite. While Microsoft Word provides basic PDF reading, WPS Office includes a dedicated PDF Editor that rivals standalone Acrobat software costing over $230/year.

Key capabilities of the WPS PDF Suite:

  • Direct Text & Image Bounding-Box Editing: Click any text box inside a PDF to edit words, fix typos, adjust font sizes, or swap image elements directly.
  • High-Fidelity PDF Conversion: Convert PDF documents into fully formatted Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), or PowerPoint (.pptx) files with zero layout shifting.
  • PDF Page Management: Split large PDF books into individual chapters, merge multiple documents into one PDF, rotate pages, or compress file size.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR): Scan physical paper receipts or image PDFs into searchable, fully editable digital documents.
  • Cryptographic Digital E-Signatures: Draw, type, or upload official signature assets to sign legal contracts and financial forms securely.

12. File Format Compatibility & Macro Support

The single biggest concern users face when switching office suites is file distortion. WPS Office addresses this challenge by providing native file reading and writing fidelity across all Microsoft OpenXML formats:

  • Word Documents: .docx, .doc, .dotx, .dot, .rtf, .txt, .html
  • Spreadsheet Workbooks: .xlsx, .xls, .xlsm, .csv, .et, .ett, .xlt
  • Presentation Decks: .pptx, .ppt, .pps, .pot, .dps, .dpt
  • PDF Documents: .pdf, .jpg, .png, .tiff, .svg

VBA & Macro Compatibility Note: Standard macros and formula scripts run smoothly in WPS Spreadsheet. However, complex legacy VBA macros that interact with proprietary Microsoft ActiveX controls require a WPS Pro or Business license.
