storefront About Alibaba

Founded in 1999 by Jack Ma and a team of 18 visionaries in Hangzhou, China, Alibaba has grown into the world's largest B2B (business-to-business) wholesale e-commerce platform. Operating under the flagship parent company Alibaba Group, the portal connects over 40 million active business buyers across 200+ countries with over 200,000 verified manufacturers, OEM/ODM suppliers, and global exporters.

Unlike traditional retail marketplaces, Alibaba specializes in bulk commercial procurement, custom product engineering, factory-direct pricing, and wholesale trade. Business owners, Amazon FBA sellers, boutique brands, and enterprise procurement officers rely on Alibaba to manufacture custom-branded merchandise, request product samples, negotiate volume discounts, and manage international freight logistics.

Whether you are looking to build a new private-label brand, source industrial equipment, or expand retail inventory, Alibaba provides the global trade infrastructure—including escrow payment protection, multi-currency trade financing, independent quality inspection services, and automated freight quotes.

verified_user Alibaba Trade Assurance Protection

Alibaba Trade Assurance is a built-in purchase protection escrow service designed to secure every B2B order from payment initiation to final delivery. When you place an order with a Trade Assurance-enabled supplier, your financial payment is deposited into a secure escrow account managed by Alibaba Citibank escrow channels.

3 Core Pillars of Trade Assurance:

  1. Payment Security: Funds are only released to the manufacturer after you confirm receipt of goods matching your agreed specifications.
  2. Product Quality Guarantee: If the delivered products fail to meet the material, dimensions, or functional standards specified in your trade contract, you can claim a partial or full refund.
  3. On-Time Shipping Guarantee: If the factory misses the contractually specified dispatch date, Alibaba compensates for shipping delays or approves order cancellation with a full refund.

shield Buyer Protection & Supplier Verification

To ensure high-trust procurement, Alibaba implements a multi-layer supplier verification system. Before placing a bulk order, buyers can check the factory's verification tier:

Gold Supplier

Paid business license verification confirming legitimate corporate registration, physical address, and contact identity.

Verified Supplier (Pro Tier)

On-site factory inspection by independent auditors (SGS, TÜV Rheinland). Includes production line capacity assessment, ISO certifications, and video tours.

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Hire accredited third-party inspectors directly via Alibaba to check goods inside the factory before container loading.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions (Alibaba FAQ)

Is Alibaba safe for wholesale sourcing? expand_more

Yes, Alibaba is highly secure when you deal with Verified Suppliers and use Trade Assurance checkout. Trade Assurance keeps your payment safe in escrow and guarantees that products meet your quality standards before funds are released.

What is Alibaba Trade Assurance and how does it protect buyers? expand_more

Trade Assurance is a free purchase protection service provided by Alibaba. It covers your order from payment through delivery, providing 100% money-back protection if product quality or shipment dates deviate from your contractual terms.

What is the difference between Alibaba and AliExpress? expand_more

Alibaba is a B2B wholesale platform meant for bulk manufacturing, custom orders, and business procurement. AliExpress is a B2C retail platform for buying single items directly. Learn more on our AliExpress Store Page.

How do Minimum Order Quantities (MOQ) work on Alibaba? expand_more

MOQ represents the smallest unit volume a manufacturer requires to initiate a production line. Many suppliers offer low MOQ sample orders (1 to 10 units) for quality testing before you commit to large production runs.
