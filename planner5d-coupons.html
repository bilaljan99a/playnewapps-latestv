5. Next-Generation AI Blueprint Recognition & Mobile AR Spatial Projection

One of Planner 5D’s most groundbreaking technological innovations is its integration of artificial intelligence and augmented reality directly into everyday spatial design workflows.

AI Plan Recognition: From Paper Sketch to 3D in Seconds

Traditionally, transforming a paper floor plan into an editable digital CAD file required hours of tedious manual measurement tracing. Planner 5D’s AI Plan Recognition engine leverages advanced computer vision neural networks trained on millions of architectural drawings.

Simply capture a photograph of your contractor’s blueprint, upload a scanned PDF, or draw a rough floor plan sketch on a napkin with room labels. The AI analyzes wall junctions, recognizes standard architectural door and window symbols, calculates proportional room scaling, and generates an interactive, fully editable 3D floor plan in seconds. Users can immediately begin dropping furniture and experimenting with interior layouts.

Augmented Reality (AR) & LiDAR Room Scanning

On iOS (iPhone/iPad with LiDAR sensors) and ARCore-compatible Android smartphones, Planner 5D transforms your mobile camera into an intelligent laser measuring tape. By scanning the corners of an empty room, the mobile app detects walls, floors, and ceiling perimeters automatically.

Once scanned, you can project virtual 1:1 scale 3D furniture into your actual living room. Wondering if an 84-inch sectional sofa will obstruct the patio doorway or if a 60-inch dining table leaves enough walking space? The AR engine displays the virtual object in real-time camera perspective, allowing you to physically walk around it in your living room before spending thousands of dollars at furniture retailers.
