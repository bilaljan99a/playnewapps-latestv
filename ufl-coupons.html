2 The "Fair to Play" Philosophy: Why UFL Rejects Scripting & Pay-to-Win

The core tenet that defines UFL's game design is the Fair to Play principle. In traditional sports simulations, players frequently experience dynamic difficulty adjustments, commonly referred to by the community as "scripting," where passes randomly misplace, defensive AI inexplicably parts ways, or goalkeepers make impossible errors to keep scorelines artificially close.

Strikerz Inc. engineered UFL around deterministic ball physics and transparent player attribute interactions:

  • Pure Skill-Based Matchmaking: Matchmaking is organized around competitive division ladders that group managers strictly by skill rating, win-loss records, and division standing rather than squad monetary value.
  • No Hidden Momentum Modifiers: When you execute a 40-yard through ball or initiate a sliding tackle in the penalty box, the outcome is determined entirely by the ball trajectory, player positioning, physical contact modeling, and input timing.
  • Meritocratic Squad Progression: While the official UFL Web Shop offers Credit Points (CP), League Points (LP), and starter packs for faster club personalization, every professional athlete in the game is earnable through consistent in-game match performance and division climb rewards.
  • Transparent Player Stats: Acceleration, sprint speed, passing vision, stamina decay, and shooting composure operate on clear, measurable numerical scales that accurately reflect pitch performance.

5 Squad Building & Club Management: How Credit Points (CP) & League Points (LP) Work

Understanding the dual-currency in-game economy of UFL is key to optimizing your club's rise through the competitive divisions. Strikerz Inc. has established two primary currencies:

CP

Credit Points (CP) — Operational Currency

Credit Points are the primary functional currency used for day-to-day club operations. Managers use CP to scout and sign professional player cards from the transfer market, pay player contract extensions, upgrade training facilities, and unlock foundational customization items. CP is earned continuously by playing ranked matches, completing daily tasks, and winning division tournaments, or purchased via Web Shop packs.

LP

League Points (LP) — Premium Currency

League Points are the premium token used for high-tier club personalization, purchasing seasonal Premium Team Passes, accelerating tier milestones, and unlocking special edition cosmetics like ambassador jerseys, custom choreos, stadium light shows, and limited-edition celebration dances.

Crucially, high-rated players in UFL do not expire through artificial annual resets. Once you sign an athlete to your club, they remain part of your permanent roster with ongoing training and tactical mastery progression.
