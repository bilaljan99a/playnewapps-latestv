About 3DLabPrint

3DLabPrint is a world-renowned pioneer in 3D-printable radio-controlled (RC) aircraft models. Founded by experienced aerospace engineers and RC pilots, 3DLabPrint develops high-fidelity STL file packages specifically engineered for standard consumer desktop FDM 3D printers.

By introducing specialized thin-wall internal ribbing and scale aerodynamic geometry, 3DLabPrint enables RC plane enthusiasts to print, assemble, and fly scale fighter planes, jets, and warbirds at a fraction of traditional composite or balsa kit costs.

tune Key Features & Specifications

  • Optimized internal wing ribbing for standard FDM 3D printers
  • Pre-configured G-code profiles for Cura, Simplify3D & PrusaSlicer
  • Scale surface detail and scale warbird paint markings
  • Instant digital STL file download access with step-by-step user manuals
