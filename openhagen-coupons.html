1. Danish Craftsmanship: The Openhagen Story

In the heart of Copenhagen, Denmark, the founders of Openhagen set out on a singular mission: to eliminate the unsightly, utilitarian black foam and industrial steel brackets that have cluttered musicians' living rooms and recording studios for decades. For generations, guitarists were forced to choose between hiding their prized vintage acoustics and custom electrics inside dark hardshell cases or resting them on bulky, clunky floor stands that occupy valuable floor real estate and present constant tipping hazards for pets and children.

Openhagen approached instrument storage not as a mere mechanical hardware problem, but as an architectural and interior design challenge. Drawing deeply upon timeless Scandinavian design principles—where functional minimalism, organic solid wood materials, and structural elegance converge—Openhagen invented HangWithMe: the world's first collapsible, magnetic solid wood guitar wall hanger that folds completely flat against the wall when your instrument is in your hands.

Every Openhagen piece is meticulously crafted using sustainably harvested, FSC-certified European Oak, American Walnut, and Smoked Oak. By pairing precision CNC woodworking with hand-finished oiled vegetable-tanned natural leather, Openhagen creates acoustic furniture that honors the instruments it cradles. Whether you are hanging a 1959 Gibson Les Paul, a Martin D-28 acoustic dreadnought, a Fender Custom Shop Stratocaster, a Kamaka Hawaiian ukulele, or a bespoke violin, Openhagen transforms your musical instruments into captivating centerpieces of architectural wall art.

3. HangWithMe Guitar Wall Mount Deep Dive

The flagship creation of the Openhagen studio is the HangWithMe wall hanger. Unlike traditional commercial guitar mounts that protrude awkwardly from drywall with exposed screw heads and industrial steel arms, HangWithMe is engineered around a patented collapsible dual-hinge mechanism.

HangWithMe Acoustic Edition – Wide Yoke for Dreadnoughts & Classical
HangWithMe Electric Edition – Narrow Yoke for Stratocasters, Teles & Basses

When you remove your guitar to practice, record, or perform, the two wooden support wings smoothly fold inward, magnetically latching against the wall plate to form a seamless, rounded Scandinavian wooden sculpture. When you return the guitar, a gentle outward pull opens the wings, automatically locking them at the exact optical angle required to securely seat your instrument headstock.

Two Precision-Engineered Sizing Profiles

  • Acoustic Model (52mm / 2.05" Yoke Width): Specially sized to comfortably accommodate wide classical guitar nuts, 12-string headstocks, and standard acoustic dreadnought necks.
  • Electric & Bass Model (44mm / 1.73" Yoke Width): Optimized for narrower electric guitar necks (Fender Stratocaster, Telecaster, Gibson Les Paul, PRS, Ibanez) and 4-to-6 string electric basses, ensuring zero side-to-side wobble.
  • Ukulele & Violin Model (32mm / 1.26" Yoke Width): Scaled down specifically for soprano, concert, tenor, and baritone ukuleles, mandolins, and classical violins.

6. Nitrocellulose Lacquer & Wood Finish Safety Explained

One of the most critical concerns for serious guitar collectors and musicians is chemical plasticizer migration. Many generic mass-market wall hangers utilize cheap synthetic foam, neoprene tubing, or thermoplastic rubber (TPR) around the yoke arms. Over time, volatile chemical plasticizers in synthetic rubbers leach into the guitar's finish, causing catastrophic chemical burning, clouding, melting, and discoloration—especially on delicate nitrocellulose lacquer found on Gibson Custom Shop, Fender American Vintage, Martin, and PRS guitars.

Openhagen eliminates this risk entirely. The contact surfaces of every HangWithMe wall mount and NordicStand floor stand are lined with 100% genuine vegetable-tanned, naturally oiled leather. Because vegetable tanning uses natural tree bark tannins rather than synthetic petroleum compounds, the leather is entirely inert, breathable, and incapable of reacting with nitrocellulose, poly, or French polish finishes. It cushions your guitar headstock with gentle organic softness while preserving your instrument's market value.
