Established in 2010 • Over 50 Million Users in 180+ Countries • High-Performance Windows Utilities

With a core mission to "Always Keep Global Data Safer", AOMEI software safeguards millions of individual users, IT administrators, and corporate networks from system crashes, accidental formatting, hardware failures, and ransomware incidents.
