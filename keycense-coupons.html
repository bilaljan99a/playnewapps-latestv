gavel Are Keycense Software Licenses 100% Legal and Genuine?

A common question among budget-conscious buyers is how Keycense can offer genuine software licenses at prices up to 70%–80% below traditional retail stores. The answer lies in European Union legal jurisprudence and wholesale bulk license distribution:

Under the landmark European Court of Justice (ECJ) ruling in Case C-128/11 (UsedSoft GmbH v. Oracle International Corp.), the principle of exhaustion of distribution rights firmly establishes that the copyright holder's exclusive distribution right is exhausted upon the initial sale of a software copy. This means that unused surplus licenses, OEM hardware-unbundled product keys, and corporate volume licenses can be legally resold and transferred to secondary buyers across the European Economic Area and globally.

Keycense adheres strictly to these international standards. Every key sold is an unused, authentic product key that activates directly against the official Microsoft, AutoDesk, or antivirus publisher activation servers without requiring third-party cracks, key generators, or unsafe patch files.
