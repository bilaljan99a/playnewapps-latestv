About Enlisted: World War II Squad-Based MMO Shooter

Enlisted is an acclaimed squad-based multiplayer online first-person shooter developed by Darkflow Software and published by Gaijin Entertainment. Unlike standard single-soldier military shooters, Enlisted places you in direct command of an entire specialized squad of historical soldiers in the most iconic theaters of World War II.

You control one soldier directly while your squad's AI follows your tactical orders (holding defensive lines, storming fortifications, placing engineering structures, or providing suppression fire). If your active soldier is eliminated, you instantly take control of the next surviving member of your squad without respawning, maintaining relentless battlefield momentum.

Yes! Enlisted is 100% free to play on PC, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 4, Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox One. You can download the client, register an account, command infantry squads, drive tanks, and fly warplanes without any required fees.

How do squad mechanics work during battle?

You command a squad of 4 to 9 soldiers. You control one soldier directly while your AI squad members support you. If your active soldier is eliminated, control switches immediately to another squad member so you stay in the fight without waiting in a spawn queue.

Can I unlock tanks and aircraft for free?

Yes! Tanks (such as Sherman, T-34, Panzer IV, Tiger) and aircraft (IL-2, Bf 109, P-51) are unlocked naturally through gameplay experience in your army research tech tree.

Does Enlisted support cross-play?

Yes, Enlisted supports cross-play matchmaking between PC, PlayStation, and Xbox consoles, with options in settings to enable or disable cross-platform matchmaking according to your preference.

Minimum requirements include a 64-bit Windows OS, Intel Core i3 or equivalent, 8 GB RAM, DirectX 11 GPU (Nvidia GeForce GTX 660 / AMD Radeon HD 7850 or higher), and 35 GB of available storage space.
