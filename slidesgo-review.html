22. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Is Slidesgo completely free to use?

Yes! Slidesgo offers thousands of 100% free presentation templates for Google Slides and PowerPoint. Free users can download and present templates by keeping the attribution credit slide intact on the final page of the presentation deck.

Q2: How does the Slidesgo AI Presentation Maker work?

The AI Presentation Maker creates complete slide decks automatically based on your text prompt. You enter a topic, select a writing tone (e.g., professional, fun, creative), pick a visual design style, and set the desired slide count. The AI writes the copy and formats the deck in under 60 seconds.

Q3: Can I edit Slidesgo templates in both Google Slides and PowerPoint?

Yes, every template on Slidesgo is engineered to be 100% compatible with both Google Slides and Microsoft PowerPoint. You can open a template directly in Google Drive or download a .pptx file for desktop PowerPoint editing.

Q4: What is the difference between Free and Premium Slidesgo accounts?

Free accounts offer limited monthly downloads and require retaining the attribution credit slide. Premium accounts provide unlimited template downloads, access to exclusive premium designs, commercial usage licensing, zero attribution requirements, and full integration with Freepik and Flaticon premium stock media.

Q5: Are Slidesgo templates safe for commercial business use?

Yes. Premium subscribers receive a full commercial license allowing them to use Slidesgo templates for business presentations, client deliverables, webinars, commercial courses, and marketing pitches without crediting Slidesgo.

Q6: Can I export Slidesgo presentation decks into Canva?

Yes. You can download Slidesgo templates as PowerPoint (.pptx) files or PDF documents and import them directly into Canva's upload workspace for online editing.

Q7: Does Slidesgo offer student and teacher education discounts?

Yes, Slidesgo provides specialized Education Plans with up to 60% discounts for teachers, students, schools, and educational institutions.

Q8: How many slide templates are available on Slidesgo?

Slidesgo currently hosts over 15,000+ professionally designed presentation templates spanning education, business, marketing, medical, creative portfolios, multi-purpose decks, and infographic libraries.

Q9: Are all elements inside Slidesgo templates fully editable?

Yes. All text placeholders, background colors, vector illustrations, icons, charts, infographics, and typography within Slidesgo templates are 100% customizable in Google Slides or PowerPoint.

Q10: How does Slidesgo integrate with Freepik and Flaticon?

Because Slidesgo is part of the Freepik Company ecosystem, its templates come pre-loaded with millions of high-resolution vector illustrations from Freepik and vector icons from Flaticon.

Q11: Can I change the aspect ratio of Slidesgo templates?

All Slidesgo templates default to the standard 16:9 widescreen format, which fits modern displays and projectors. You can also re-scale decks to 4:3 standard or custom dimensions inside Google Slides or PowerPoint settings.

Q12: How does Slidesgo compare to Canva?

Canva is a full graphic design suite, whereas Slidesgo specializes specifically in structured presentation decks for Google Slides and PowerPoint. Slidesgo offers more structured educational and business presentation structures.

Q13: How does Slidesgo compare to Gamma AI and Beautiful.ai?

Gamma AI and Beautiful.ai use proprietary web editors with rigid formatting rules. Slidesgo gives you complete creative freedom by generating or providing slide decks that open directly in universal software like Google Slides and PowerPoint.

Q14: Is credit card required for the Slidesgo free plan?

No credit card is required to sign up for a free Slidesgo account and download free presentation templates.

Q15: What payment methods does Slidesgo accept for Premium plans?

Slidesgo accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, and invoice options for school or enterprise team licenses.
