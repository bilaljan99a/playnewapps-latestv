help 3. How much compensation can I get for a delayed flight through Lennuabi?

Under EU Regulation EC 261/2004, compensation amounts are legally fixed based on flight distance: €250 per passenger for short-haul flights (up to 1,500 km), €400 for medium-haul flights (1,500 km to 3,500 km), and €600 for long-haul flights (over 3,500 km) delayed by 4+ hours.

help 4. What documents are needed to file an EC 261 claim with Lennuabi?

To file a flight compensation claim with Lennuabi, you need your flight booking reference (PNR), e-ticket or boarding passes, and a valid photo ID.

help 5. What flight distance determines EC 261 compensation amounts?

Compensation amounts under EU EC 261/2004 are legally set: €250 for flights under 1,500 km, €400 for intra-EU flights over 1,500 km and other flights between 1,500 km and 3,500 km, and €600 for non-internal EU flights over 3,500 km.

help 9. Can I claim compensation for an old flight from 2 or 3 years ago?

Yes! In many European countries, the legal statute of limitations for EC261 claims ranges from 3 to 6 years (e.g., 3 years in Estonia and Germany, 6 years in the UK). You can check old flight numbers for free on Lennuabi.

help 10. What are extraordinary circumstances in flight delay claims?

Extraordinary circumstances are events outside an airline's operational control, such as extreme weather, volcanic ash, political instability, or air traffic control strikes. Technical maintenance failures, airline crew shortages, and operational delay cascades are NOT extraordinary circumstances.

help 11. Does Lennuabi handle missed connecting flights?

help 12. What if the airline already gave me food vouchers or hotel stays?

Food vouchers, hotel accommodations, and airport transport provided during a delay represent the airline's statutory "duty of care." Accepting care vouchers does NOT forfeit your right to monetary EC261 compensation (€250–€600).

13. Airport Disruption Action Plan Checklist for Travelers

Essential steps to take immediately when your flight is delayed or canceled at the airport

If you are currently experiencing a flight disruption at an airport or have recently landed after a severe delay, taking these immediate steps will significantly strengthen your compensation claim with Lennuabi:

1. Keep All Boarding Passes & Electronic Tickets Never throw away paper boarding passes or delete mobile wallet flight passes. Keep booking confirmation emails containing your 6-character PNR code.
2. Request Written Delay Reason From Gate Staff Ask the airport gate agents to state the official cause of delay on record. Take a photo of airport flight status monitors showing delay timestamps.
3. Retain Receipts for Meal & Hotel Expenses If a delay exceeds 2 hours, the airline must provide food and drink. If you pay out-of-pocket, keep itemized receipts for reimbursement.
4. Note Exact Gate Door Opening Time Under EC261 law, delay duration is measured when the aircraft door opens at the arrival gate and passengers are permitted to exit.
