rocket_launch About Star Conflict: Tactical Space MMO Combat

Star Conflict is an action-oriented, free-to-play massively multiplayer space combat simulation game co-developed by Star Gem Inc. / Targem Games and published by Gaijin Entertainment. Set three thousand years in the future across Sector 1337 — a war-torn frontier galaxy rich with remnants of an ancient Precursor civilization — pilots take direct control of customizable starships to battle for supremacy, valuable alien artifacts, and planetary trade routes.

With full 6-degrees-of-freedom flight physics, over a hundred unique starships across three rival factions, massive Open Space sectors, and cooperative PvE raids, Star Conflict delivers fast-paced tactical dogfighting combined with deep RPG-style module crafting and fleet progression.

groups The Three Rival Factions of Sector 1337

Pilots in Star Conflict align with one of three major human factions, each featuring proprietary propulsion technologies, tactical hull designs, and weapon synergies:

shield

The Empire

The militaristic descendant of old Earth. Empire ships prioritize thick hull plating, long-range thermal laser weaponry, and crushing firepower. They field unmatched Guard Frigates and heavy Gunships.

speed

The Federation

An alliance of autonomous merchant worlds and rebel syndicates. Federation vessels utilize lightweight alloys, plasma cannons, and unmatched speed and maneuverability for rapid flanking strikes.

psychology

Jericho

A cybernetically enhanced human clan governed by techno-religious families. Jericho crafts ships with powerful multi-layered energy shields, guided missile swarms, and electronic warfare suites.
