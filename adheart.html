About Adheart: The Premier Ad Intelligence Platform

In the competitive landscape of modern performance marketing and digital media buying, creative fatigue is the single greatest obstacle to sustained campaign profitability. Modern advertising algorithms on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok heavily prioritize ad creative freshness, hook engagement, and user retention. To remain competitive, media buyers, e-commerce brands, and affiliate marketers must continuously analyze high-performing ad angles, visual hooks, and landing page funnels.

Adheart is an industry-standard ad intelligence and spy platform designed specifically to solve creative discovery friction. By indexing over 900 million global ad creatives across Meta platforms and TikTok, Adheart provides an active window into global digital advertising trends, competitor spend patterns, and winning direct-response offers.

Why Marketers & Media Buyers Rely on Adheart

Traditional manual ad research via native public ad libraries (such as the standard Facebook Ad Library) suffers from severe functional limitations: lack of advanced keyword filtering, inability to filter by ad duration or running longevity, non-exportable media files, and absence of structured performance metrics. Adheart overcomes these bottlenecks by offering a dedicated search engine built for performance advertisers.

  • Instant Creative Longevity Validation: Filter ad campaigns running continuously for 30, 60, or 90+ days. If an advertiser continues spending money on an ad creative for months, it is a verified winning creative generating positive ROI.
  • Native Video & Image Exporter: Download original 1080p high-definition MP4 video creatives and high-resolution carousels directly to your computer without watermarks or quality loss.
  • Complete Funnel Transparency: Inspect every element of a competitor's funnel—from the initial Facebook/TikTok video ad hook to the exact Shopify landing page, product offer price, checkout upsell, and thank-you page.
  • Global GEO & Language Targeting: Spy on competitor campaigns running in over 150 countries, enabling international scale and cross-border e-commerce expansion.
