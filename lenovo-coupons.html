devices Comprehensive Lenovo Product Ecosystem & Model Guide

ThinkPad The Gold Standard in Business Computing

Distinguished by the legendary TrackPoint red navigation button, ergonomic spill-resistant keyboards, and MIL-STD 810H military certification, the ThinkPad family is the undisputed benchmark for enterprise security and road-warrior durability. The flagship ThinkPad X1 Carbon delivers an impossibly thin carbon-fiber chassis weighing under 2.5 lbs, while the ThinkPad T-Series (such as the T14 and T16) serves as the dependable workhorse for corporate enterprise fleets. For engineering, 3D animation, and scientific simulations, the ThinkPad P-Series mobile workstations pack ISV-certified NVIDIA RTX discrete graphics and ECC memory support. Budget-conscious small businesses and startups turn to the ThinkPad E-Series and L-Series for enterprise-grade security at accessible price points.

Yoga Premium 2-in-1s & Next-Gen Copilot+ AI PCs

Lenovo invented the 360-degree convertible laptop category with the Yoga series. Today, the Yoga lineup sets design milestones with rotating Bowers & Wilkins soundbar hinges, vibrant PureSight 3K and 4K OLED touch displays, and precision stylus pen integration. The groundbreaking Yoga Book 9i offers dual full-sized 13.3-inch OLED displays for unprecedented mobile multitasking, while the cutting-edge Yoga Slim 7x integrates Snapdragon X Elite silicon delivering dedicated 45 TOPS NPU power for next-generation Windows Copilot+ AI experiences, silent fanless operation, and multi-day battery life.

Legion & LOQ High-Performance Gaming, Handhelds & Esports

Engineered for zero-compromise performance, Lenovo Legion laptops and towers (Legion Pro 7i, Legion 5i, Legion Slim 5) dominate esports tournaments with Coldfront 5.0 vapor-chamber cooling, AI-tuned power distribution, ultra-responsive TrueStrike per-key RGB keyboards, and 240Hz PureSight Gaming displays powered by NVIDIA GeForce RTX 40-Series and AMD Radeon graphics. For gamers on the go, the Legion Go handheld console delivers desktop PC versatility in an 8.8-inch 144Hz handheld form factor with detachable ergonomic controllers and FPS mode mouse navigation. For value-driven gamers, the Lenovo LOQ line offers robust thermal headroom and discrete GPUs at budget-friendly price points.

ThinkBook & IdeaPad Modern Professional & Everyday Productivity

Bridging the gap between consumer flair and corporate security, ThinkBook laptops (such as the ThinkBook 14x and 16p) feature dual-tone mineral gray aluminum finishes, smart noise-cancelling microphones, and biometric fingerprint power buttons tailored for modern professionals and digital agencies. Meanwhile, IdeaPad Slim and Flex notebooks provide unbeatable value for students, families, and everyday office work, packing high-definition IPS displays, Rapid Charge Boost battery technology, and physical webcam privacy shutters.

Desktops & Gear Workstations, Tiny PCs, ThinkVision & Thunderbolt Docks

Lenovo’s desktop footprint includes ThinkCentre M-Series Tiny PCs—1-liter ultra-compact computing modules that hide behind monitors while powering enterprise trading floors and medical facilities. For serious creative workstations, ThinkStation P-Series towers feature chassis designed in collaboration with Aston Martin for optimized acoustic aerodynamics. Pair your system with ThinkVision factory-calibrated 4K color displays, single-cable Thunderbolt 4 universal docking stations, and ergonomic wireless accessories to create the ultimate clutter-free workspace.
