1. Overview & Monolith Industrial Design

Microsoft entered the current console generation with a singular hardware design goal: build the most technologically uncompromising, whisper-quiet home console without resorting to garish styling. The result is the Xbox Series X—a sleek, matte-black rectangular monolithic tower measuring 151mm x 151mm x 301mm and weighing 4.45 kg.

Unlike the PlayStation 5’s polarizing, organic curves and large footprint, the Series X blends seamlessly into modern living room entertainment centers, looking more like an architectural subwoofer or a high-end desktop audio DAC than a gaming toy.

2. 12 TFLOPS Architecture, Split Motherboard & Whisper-Quiet Cooling

Under the hood, Microsoft engineered a revolutionary split motherboard architecture. By placing the CPU/GPU silicon on one board and the storage/power regulation on a parallel board sandwiching a heavy aluminum chassis, heat is efficiently transferred to a central vapor chamber.

A single, custom-designed 130mm axial fan draws cool air from the bottom vents and pulls it through the vertical thermal channel, exhausting warm air out the distinctive green-accented concave top mesh. In our lab testing under sustained 4K 120Hz loads in Forza Motorsport:

  • Acoustic Noise: The Series X registered a near-silent 24.2 dB at 1 meter. It is virtually indistinguishable from ambient room silence.
  • Peak Power Draw: Consistently averaged 155W to 205W during heavy 4K ray tracing gameplay.

3. Quick Resume: The Single Greatest Quality-of-Life Feature in Modern Gaming

While both PS5 and Series X feature high-speed NVMe solid-state drives, Microsoft leveraged its Windows kernel virtualization expertise to create Quick Resume.

When you launch a new game, the Series X does not force-close your existing title. Instead, it writes a compressed image of the active system RAM (approx. 13GB) directly to a dedicated partition of the internal SSD in less than 3 seconds. You can have 5 to 8 massive open-world titles simultaneously suspended. In our tests, switching from Cyberpunk 2077 directly into Forza Horizon 5 took only 4.8 seconds, placing you precisely where you paused with zero loading screens.

5. Xbox Game Pass Ultimate: The Unrivaled Value in Gaming

Buying an Xbox Series X without Xbox Game Pass Ultimate ($19.99/mo) is like buying an iPhone without the App Store. Game Pass transforms the console into an all-inclusive gaming buffet featuring:

  • Day-One First-Party Games: Every single game from Xbox Game Studios, Bethesda, Activision-Blizzard, and Obsidian (including Call of Duty, Doom: The Dark Ages, Avowed, Fable, Indiana Jones, Starfield) launches directly on Game Pass at no additional charge.
  • EA Play & Ubisoft+ Inclusion: Full access to the EA Play vault (FIFA/FC, Madden, Battlefield, Dead Space) and cloud saves.
  • Xbox Cloud Gaming (xCloud): Stream games directly to your phone, tablet, smart TV, or Steam Deck with cross-progression.

6. Four-Generation Backward Compatibility & FPS Boost

Microsoft’s backward compatibility engineering team deserves immense praise. Unlike Sony (which requires cloud streaming for PS3 titles), the Series X runs thousands of games from the Original Xbox (2001), Xbox 360 (2005), and Xbox One (2013) natively from discs or digital licenses.

Through FPS Boost and Auto HDR, older classics like Fallout: New Vegas, Skate 3, Gears of War 2, Dead Space 2, and Red Dead Redemption run at rock-solid 60 FPS or 120 FPS with 16x anisotropic filtering and 4K native rendering—breathing new life into your vintage game collection.

thumb_down Limitations to Know (Cons)

9. Xbox Series X vs. Series S: Which Console Should You Buy?

Microsoft also offers the digital-only Xbox Series S ($299). If you game on a 1080p bedroom TV or monitor and want an affordable Game Pass machine, the Series S is great value.

However, if you own a 4K HDR television or 120Hz display, the Xbox Series X ($499) is worth every single extra dollar. You get 3x the GPU compute power, a full 4K Ultra HD Blu-ray disc drive for cheap physical used games, double the RAM (16GB vs 10GB), and double the base high-speed storage (1TB vs 512GB).

10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Xbox Series X include a 4K Blu-ray drive?

Yes. The Series X includes an Ultra HD 4K Blu-ray physical disc drive that supports 4K movie discs, physical game discs, and legacy backward compatible discs.

How do you expand storage on Xbox Series X?

You can expand high-speed internal NVMe storage using official Seagate or Western Digital Storage Expansion Cards (512GB, 1TB, or 2TB) that plug directly into the dedicated rear slot. For older backward compatible Xbox 360/One games, you can use any standard external USB 3.0 hard drive.

Does Xbox Series X support Discord and Apple Music?

Yes. Xbox Series X has full native Discord voice chat integration with direct stream sharing, as well as background audio playback via Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube Music.
