8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do character credits work in ElevenLabs?

One generated English letter, space, or punctuation symbol equals one character credit. On average, 1,000 characters corresponds to approximately 150 to 200 spoken words (~1 minute of continuous speech). If you synthesize a 3,000-character script, exactly 3,000 credits are deducted from your monthly quota.

Do unused monthly characters roll over to the next month?

Monthly character quotas reset at the beginning of each billing cycle and do not roll over. If your project demands exceed your plan quota, you can enable usage-based character top-ups or upgrade your tier mid-cycle with prorated billing.

Can I cancel my ElevenLabs subscription anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through your Account Settings with one click. You will retain full access to your plan's character balance and cloned voice slots until the end of your current billing period, with zero cancellation penalties.

Does ElevenLabs offer student or non-profit discounts?
