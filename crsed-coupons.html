CRSED: F.O.A.D. Store & Game Summary

CRSED: F.O.A.D. (originally launched as Cuisine Royale and officially titled Cuisine Royale Second Edition: Fulfillment of All Desires) is a brutal, high-octane tactical Battle Royale developed by Darkflow Software and published globally by Gaijin Entertainment. Combining hyper-realistic firearm ballistics simulated in the renowned Dagor Engine with supernatural mystical rituals, blood sacrifices, demonic traps, and signature kitchenware armor, CRSED delivers one of the most distinctive and adrenaline-charged survival experiences in online competitive gaming.

1 The Evolution: From April Fools Parody to Hardcore Tactical Titan

Official CRSED: F.O.A.D. Key Art depicting supernatural champions wielding realistic WWII and modern firearms alongside mystical signs.

In the crowded landscape of modern battle royales, few games boast an origin story as unique and fascinating as CRSED: F.O.A.D.. Originally launched on April Fools' Day 2018 under the comedic moniker Cuisine Royale by Darkflow Software, the game was conceived as a lighthearted parody of military survival shooters like PlayerUnknown’s Battlegrounds (PUBG). Players spawned into vast European landscapes wearing boxers, strapping heavy waffle irons to their chests as makeshift ballistic vests, and balancing colanders and roasting pans on their heads for helmet protection.

However, what began as an elaborate joke quickly captivated hundreds of thousands of competitive shooter enthusiasts. Underneath the satirical kitchenware was an exceptionally robust, physics-accurate tactical shooter powered by Gaijin Entertainment’s celebrated Dagor Engine—the same military-grade simulation engine that powers the world-famous vehicle combat sim War Thunder. Gunplay felt visceral, bullet velocity and ballistic drop mirrored real-world physics, and the netcode delivered crisp, responsive hit registration.

Recognizing the immense competitive potential of their creation, Darkflow Software transformed the project. They expanded the core gunplay with deep occult lore, brutal blood sacrifice mechanics, and diverse Champion archetypes wielding supernatural superpowers. In late 2020, the game officially rebranded as CRSED: F.O.A.D. (Cuisine Royale Second Edition: Fulfillment of All Desires). Today, CRSED stands as a premier free-to-play tactical battle royale that strikes the perfect balance between serious military gunplay, intense occult strategy, and dark, irreverent humor.

2 Dagor Engine Realism & Supernatural Hybrid Combat

True Ballistic Physics: Bullet drop, muzzle velocity, and surface penetration are computed in real time.
Supernatural Rituals: Summon eclipses, zombie apocalypses, and gravitational anomalies.

What truly distinguishes CRSED: F.O.A.D. from other arcade shooters is its underlying simulation DNA. Every bullet fired in CRSED is a physical projectile calculated with precise muzzle velocity, atmospheric drag, trajectory drop, and terminal energy retention. Firing an anti-tank rifle like the PTRS-41 across an open field in Normandy requires understanding target leading, distance compensation, and structural penetration—thin wooden fences and sheet metal will not stop high-caliber rounds from devastating enemies taking cover behind them.

CRSED layers a groundbreaking mystical soul economy directly on top of this military-grade realism:

  • Sinister Souls Harvesting: Every time you eliminate an opponent, deal heavy damage, or smash occult gravestones scattered across the map, you collect glowing Sinister Souls. Souls act as the fuel for your champion's active supernatural abilities, ritual summoning, and mystical combat signs.
  • High-Stakes Resource Balancing: Do you hoard your Sinister Souls to unleash a game-altering map-wide Eclipse ritual during the final circle collapse, or do you spend them immediately on tactical jump pads, teleportation traps, and self-healing hex bags?
  • First-Person & Third-Person Flexibility: CRSED provides seamless toggling between tactical third-person perspective (ideal for spatial situational awareness) and precise first-person aim down sights (ADS) with authentic iron sights, red dots, holographic sights, and high-power sniper optics.

12 Frequently Asked Questions (CRSED: F.O.A.D. FAQ)

Is CRSED: F.O.A.D. really 100% free to play?

Yes, absolutely. You can download, install, and play CRSED completely free of charge on all platforms (PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X/S, and Switch). You have complete access to all maps, weapons, vehicles, rituals, and matchmaking servers without ever entering a credit card.

Why are players wearing pots, pans, and colanders as armor?

CRSED began as Cuisine Royale, a comical April Fools battle royale where players wore kitchenware as makeshift armor. While the game has evolved into a serious supernatural shooter with powerful champions, the iconic kitchenware armor remains as a signature stylistic mechanic. Woks provide heavy torso protection, while colanders protect your head against fatal sniper shots.

How do I unlock new Champions without paying?

You unlock new Champions by collecting in-game summoning scrolls and spending Sinister Souls earned by scoring eliminations, placing high in matches, and completing daily battle pass challenges.

Does CRSED support crossplay between PC and consoles?

Yes! Full cross-platform multiplayer matchmaking allows PC players, PlayStation gamers, Xbox players, and Nintendo Switch users to squad up in the same matches together. You can toggle crossplay on or off in the settings menu.

Are there bots in CRSED: F.O.A.D. matches?

New players who are just starting out in their first few matches will face automated training bots to help them learn movement, looting, and shooting mechanics. Once you climb the player ranking ladder, matches are populated with real competitive players.

How does the Sinister Souls mechanic work?

During a match, dealing damage, eliminating foes, and breaking tombstones rewards you with glowing Sinister Souls. These souls act as an in-match mystical energy pool used to cast champion superpowers, trigger global weather rituals (like Solar Eclipse or Low Gravity), and place trap runes on the ground.
