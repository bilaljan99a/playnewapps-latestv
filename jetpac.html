2. What is Jetpac eSIM?

Jetpac is a global travel technology platform and mobile virtual network operator (MVNO) designed specifically to eliminate physical SIM card swapping and unpredictable roaming bills. Built directly into iOS and Android smartphones equipped with eSIM chips, Jetpac enables users to purchase, provision, and activate local data subscriptions in a matter of seconds.

Unlike legacy travel SIM providers that force users to delete and reinstall a new eSIM profile for every new destination visited, Jetpac utilizes a reusable single eSIM architecture. Once you install the Jetpac profile on your device for your first trip, that same profile remains stored safely on your phone. Whenever you travel to a new country or require additional high-speed gigabytes, you simply top up or select a new regional pass directly inside the intuitive Jetpac iOS or Android application.

Jetpac contracts directly with premier tier-1 local telecommunication carriers worldwide—such as NTT Docomo and SoftBank in Japan, Vodafone and Orange across Europe, and T-Mobile and AT&T in the United States—ensuring that subscribers enjoy low-latency, high-priority cellular bandwidth wherever their journeys lead.

14. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I know if my smartphone supports Jetpac eSIM?

A: Most smartphones manufactured since 2019 support eSIM technology. This includes iPhone XS, iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, Google Pixel 3 and newer, Samsung Galaxy S20 through S24, and modern flagship Android devices. You can verify by dialing *#06# on your phone keypad—if an EID number appears, your phone is eSIM compatible.

Q: How do I claim my free SmartDelay airport lounge voucher?

A: Simply open the Jetpac app, navigate to the SmartDelay tab, and enter your airline flight number at least 24 hours prior to departure. If your flight experiences a delay of 60 minutes or longer, Jetpac sends a LoungeKey PDF voucher directly to your app and registered email.

Q: Can I keep my original physical SIM active for phone calls?

A: Yes! Modern smartphones support Dual SIM Dual Standby (DSDS). You can keep your primary SIM active to receive SMS text messages or urgent calls while setting Jetpac eSIM as your designated line for all Cellular Data.

Q: Do Jetpac data packages expire if I don't use them immediately?

A: Your data validity period begins only when your eSIM connects to a supported local cellular network at your destination. You can safely purchase and install your Jetpac plan days or weeks before your trip.
