How do I choose the correct size for Brazilian shapewear?

Never guess your size based on standard American or European dress tags. Brazilian shapewear is engineered based on exact anatomical centimeter measurements. Take a soft measuring tape and measure your natural waistline (at the narrowest point right above your belly button) and your widest hip circumference. Compare your measurements directly to the size chart on each product page. If you fall between two sizes, choose the larger size for everyday comfort or the smaller size for maximum tight compression.

Are Metro Brazil products 100% authentic?

Yes, 100%. Metro Brazil is the official authorized global distribution partner for Brazil's premier luxury textile brands, including Plié, Lupo, and Liz. All items arrive with original factory packaging, holographic authenticity tags, and Brazilian textile certification labels.

How should I wash and care for my Brazilian corset and shapewear?

To preserve the elasticity of the hydrophilic microfiber and protect the galvanized spiral steel boning, always hand wash your garments in cold or lukewarm water using a mild, bleach-free detergent. Never place shapewear into an electric dryer, as high heat degrades elastane bonds. Gently squeeze out excess water in a towel and lay the garment flat to air dry in the shade.

Can I wear a Metro Brazil waist cincher while working out?

Plié and Lupo design specific activewear waist trainers crafted with breathable, perforated hydro-polyamide that safely supports your core and lumbar spine during light cardio, walking, and weightlifting. However, avoid wearing tightly boned formal corsets during high-intensity aerobics or inverted abdominal exercises.

Yes. The Brazilian hair care products curated on Metro Brazil are formulated without harsh formaldehyde.
