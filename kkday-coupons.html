settings_cell Complete Guide: How to Install and Activate KKday Travel eSIMs

Digital eSIMs purchased on KKday provide seamless, high-speed mobile data without the inconvenience of physical SIM trays or expensive domestic roaming surcharges. Follow this standardized procedure for smooth activation:

Step 1
Verify Phone Compatibility and Carrier Lock Status: Before purchasing, confirm your handset is unlocked by your home network carrier and supports digital eSIM technology. On iPhone, check Settings > General > About > Carrier Lock (it must display "No SIM restrictions").
Step 2
Scan the QR Code Prior to Boarding Your Flight: While connected to a reliable home or airport Wi-Fi network, open your device camera or navigate to Settings > Cellular / Mobile Data > Add eSIM. Scan the official QR code sent to your email by KKday. When prompted, label the profile "Travel" or "KKday" to distinguish it from your primary line.
Step 3
Enable Data Roaming Upon Arrival at Destination: Once your aircraft lands at your overseas destination, switch your phone's Cellular Data line to the "KKday" profile and toggle Data Roaming to ON for this specific eSIM profile. Leave Data Roaming OFF for your domestic primary line to prevent unexpected home telco charges. Your phone will register with local partner networks (e.g., SoftBank/Docomo in Japan) within 60 to 120 seconds.
