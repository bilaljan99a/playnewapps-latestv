corporate_fare About Fiverr

Founded in 2010, Fiverr revolutionized the global gig economy by creating an open digital marketplace where businesses and individuals can buy and sell digital services instantly. Headquartered in Tel Aviv with hubs worldwide, Fiverr connects millions of buyers with talented freelancers across more than 160 countries.

From logo design and full-stack software engineering to AI prompt generation, voiceover recording, and digital marketing campaigns, Fiverr streamlines contract execution. Buyers browse cataloged "Gigs" with upfront pricing, clear deliverables, and defined delivery times—eliminating traditional bidding hassles.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fiverr and how does it work?

Fiverr is an online marketplace connecting buyers with freelance service providers offering cataloged "Gigs" across programming, graphics design, digital marketing, AI services, copywriting, and video production.

What is the difference between standard Fiverr, Fiverr Pro, and Fiverr Business?

Standard Fiverr provides access to millions of freelancers starting at $5. Fiverr Pro features hand-vetted, top 1% industry talent. Fiverr Business offers team collaboration tools, shared payment methods, and dedicated account management.

How does buyer protection work on Fiverr?

Fiverr holds payments safely in escrow. Sellers are only paid after the buyer reviews and approves the delivered work according to project requirements.

Does Fiverr offer a discount or referral program?

Yes, through Fiverr's Referral Program, invited friends receive 10% OFF their first Fiverr order, while referrers can earn Fiverr Credits up to US$500 total.
