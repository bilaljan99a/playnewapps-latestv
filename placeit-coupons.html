help_outline Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is Placeit by Envato?

Placeit by Envato is an all-in-one online mockup generator, vector logo creator, social media graphic maker, and video editor offering over 100,000 smart browser-based templates with full commercial usage rights.

Can I use Placeit mockups and designs to sell products on Etsy and Shopify?

Yes. Placeit provides a comprehensive, royalty-free commercial license on all downloaded graphics, mockups, and logos. You can use exported mockups as product listing images on Etsy, Amazon Merch, Shopify, Redbubble, and WooCommerce, or sell printed physical apparel containing Placeit graphics.

Do I need Photoshop or any design software to use Placeit?

No. Placeit operates 100% in your web browser. You upload your flat artwork, choose your colors, edit typography, and Placeit's cloud servers handle all 3D warping, shadow displacement, and high-resolution rendering automatically.

What is the difference between Placeit Monthly and Annual plans?

The Monthly plan costs $14.95 per month with cancel-anytime flexibility. The Annual plan costs $89.69 per year (equivalent to ~$7.47 per month), delivering a 50% discount compared to paying monthly over twelve months.

What happens to my downloaded assets if I cancel my subscription?

You retain perpetual commercial rights to all assets downloaded during your active subscription period. You can continue using and selling those mockups, logos, and videos indefinitely without paying ongoing fees.
