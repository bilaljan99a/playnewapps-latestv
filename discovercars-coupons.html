5 The Pickup and Drop-off Process: What to Do at the Rental Desk

Arriving prepared at the rental desk ensures you get your keys quickly and avoid common counter disputes. Follow this practical checklist:

1. Documents You Must Present at the Counter

• Printed or digital DiscoverCars Rental Voucher.
• Original, valid Physical Driving License (held for at least 1–2 full years).
• Passport or National ID Card matching the primary driver's name.
• Physical Credit Card in the primary driver's name with sufficient available limit for the security deposit.

2. Inspecting the Vehicle & Taking Video Evidence

Before driving out of the parking bay, spend 2 minutes conducting a thorough walkaround. Use your smartphone to take a continuous 360-degree video and close-up photos of all existing scratches, scuffs on wheel rims, windshield chips, and interior condition. Check that any pre-existing imperfections are noted on the supplier's checkout condition report.

3. Drop-off Checklist & Fuel Verification

Refuel the car at a petrol station within 10 km (6 miles) of the return facility if your contract specifies a Full-to-Full policy. Keep the printed fuel receipt. Take a final photo of the dashboard showing the fuel gauge and total odometer reading when parking the car at the return bay.

6 Rental Requirements: Age Limitations, Young Driver Surcharges & Driving Permits

Every rental company establishes specific eligibility criteria governed by local traffic laws and fleet insurance underwriters. Here are the core rules to know:

  • Minimum Driving Age: In most countries, the minimum age to rent a car is 21 years old, although certain suppliers in Germany, Austria, and the UK permit rentals from age 18 or 19 for economy class vehicles.
  • Young Driver Surcharges (Under 25): Drivers aged 18 to 24 typically incur a mandatory daily "Young Driver Fee" imposed by the local supplier (ranging from $10 to $30/day). DiscoverCars displays this fee clearly in the Rental Conditions.
  • Senior Driver Policies (Over 70): In certain countries (such as Ireland, Greece, and Malta), drivers over 70–75 years old may face upper age restrictions or senior driver surcharges.
  • License Tenure: Nearly all suppliers require primary drivers to have held their full, valid domestic driving license for a minimum of 12 continuous months (or 24 months for luxury vehicles).
  • International Driving Permit (IDP): An IDP is an official multilingual translation of your domestic driving license. If your license was issued in a non-Latin script, or if you are renting in countries strictly requiring it (such as Italy, Austria, Greece, Japan, or international visitors in certain US states), you must carry both your physical IDP and your original domestic license.

7 Insurance & Protection Options: DiscoverCars Full Coverage vs. Rental Counter CDW

Understanding car rental insurance is essential to avoid overpaying. Every car rented through DiscoverCars comes standard with compulsory basic coverage as required by law in the destination country:

  • Third-Party Liability (TPL): Covers injury or property damage caused to other people and their vehicles.
  • Collision Damage Waiver (CDW): Limits your financial liability for bodywork damage down to a specified "Deductible" or "Excess" amount (typically $1,000 to $2,500).
  • Theft Protection (TP): Caps your financial loss if the vehicle is stolen.

However, basic CDW does not cover high-risk components like glass, windshields, tires, wheels, mirrors, roof, undercarriage, locks, towing, or administrative fees. Rental desk agents routinely exploit this gap by aggressively pitching "Super CDW" or "Zero Excess" counter insurance at steep daily rates ($25 to $45/day).

8 Security Deposits, Payment Methods & Credit Card Considerations

One of the most frequent points of confusion for travelers is the difference between paying for the rental reservation and providing a security deposit at the counter:

payment Online Booking Payment

When reserving your vehicle on DiscoverCars, you can pay using any major credit card, debit card (Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit), or supported digital wallets. DiscoverCars charges 0% payment processing surcharges.

credit_card Counter Security Deposit Hold

When you arrive at the pickup desk, the supplier requires a temporary pre-authorization hold (security deposit) on a physical credit card in the primary driver's exact name. This hold covers potential fuel shortages, unpaid tolls, traffic fines, or damage excess.

Can I Rent with a Debit Card?
Yes! While traditional rental companies strictly mandate credit cards, DiscoverCars features a dedicated filter labeled "Debit Card for Deposit". Selecting this filter displays suppliers that accept Visa or Mastercard debit cards for the counter deposit hold. Be aware that debit card deposits require the physical funds to be temporarily debited from your bank account balance and refunded 10–20 days following vehicle return.

11 International Car Rental Tips: Cross-Border Driving, Tolls & Fuel Policies

When driving in a foreign country, keep these crucial operational rules in mind:

  • Cross-Border Driving Permissions: If you plan to drive your rental car across international borders (e.g., picking up in Munich and driving to Austria or Switzerland), you must declare this during booking or at the desk. Suppliers issue a "Cross-Border Authorization" and provide necessary cross-border vehicle insurance.
  • Highway Vignettes & Toll Transponders: Several European countries (Austria, Switzerland, Slovenia, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary) require an electronic highway toll sticker ("Vignette") to drive on motorways. Driving without a valid vignette carries heavy automated camera fines. In countries like Portugal and Italy, ask your rental supplier for an electronic toll transponder (Via Verde / Telepass).
  • Restricted Traffic Zones (ZTLs): Many historic Italian and Spanish cities enforce strict Limited Traffic Zones (Zona a Traffico Limitato / ZTL) monitored by automated cameras. Never drive into a historic city center without hotel authorization.
  • Always Choose "Full-to-Full" Fuel Policy: With a Full-to-Full policy, you receive the car with a full tank of fuel and return it full. This avoids the predatory refueling markups charged under "Pre-purchase Fuel" options.

12 How to Find the Best DiscoverCars Deals & Maximize Your Savings

1. Book 2–8 Weeks Ahead for Peak Seasons

Rental car pricing operates on dynamic yield management. Reserving 2–8 weeks in advance locks in early bird inventory, especially for summer travel across Spain, Italy, Portugal, and Greece.

2. Check 7-Day vs. 5-Day Weekly Rates

Many suppliers offer discounted weekly rate tiers. Booking a car for 7 full days can sometimes cost equal to or less than renting for 5 individual days.

3. Re-Check Rates Before the 48h Window

Because DiscoverCars offers 100% free cancellation up to 48 hours prior to pickup, you can re-check prices 3–4 days before departure. If prices drop, rebook at the lower rate and cancel the old reservation with zero fee.

4. Bring Your Own Child Safety Seats

Rental desks often charge $10 to $15/day per child safety seat. Most international airlines check car seats free of charge, saving you $70–$150 on a 10-day trip.

14 DiscoverCars Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is DiscoverCars a legitimate and safe website to book car rentals?

How do I submit a claim under DiscoverCars Full Coverage?

What happens if my flight is delayed?

Always provide your flight number when completing your DiscoverCars booking. Rental counter staff track incoming flights and will hold your reserved vehicle if your flight experiences delays. If a major delay occurs, you can also contact DiscoverCars' 24/7 multilingual support team.

Can I add extra drivers to my reservation?

Yes. Many promotional offers on DiscoverCars include a "Free Additional Driver." If an additional driver is not included in the base rate, you can add them during checkout or directly at the supplier's pickup desk upon presenting their valid driving license and passport.

Do I need to clean the car before returning it?

Normal driving dust and minor floor dirt are expected and do not require professional detailing. However, returning a vehicle with heavy interior mud, beach sand, pet hair, or food stains will trigger a mandatory supplier valeting fee ($50–$150). Remove all personal garbage and shake out floor mats before return.
